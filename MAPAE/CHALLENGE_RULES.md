# MAPAE Challenge Rules

## What is invited

Participants may lawfully download, copy, index, parse, validate, compare, statistically analyze, model, search, and otherwise study the published **public-address material**.

The project specifically invites reproducible evidence of:

- duplicate or malformed addresses;
- checksum or witness-program defects;
- repeated decoded witness programs;
- detectable correlations or generation structure;
- statistical anomalies;
- links to previously known public addresses;
- address reuse or blockchain-history findings;
- reproducible inference about the generation process;
- or any demonstrable ability to authorize a spend from a challenge address without being supplied its private key.

## What is not invited

The project does not authorize:

- unauthorized access to any computer, account, network, wallet, or service;
- malware, credential theft, phishing, social engineering, impersonation, or physical intrusion;
- denial-of-service activity;
- platform abuse, spam, fake accounts, fabricated transactions, or circumvention of service rules;
- attempts to obtain unpublished secrets from people or systems;
- targeting unrelated third parties.

The published public-address material is the research target. Third-party infrastructure is not.

## Evidence standard

A report should include:

1. the exact corpus or subset version and SHA-256;
2. the affected address or source row where available;
3. reproducible code, commands, or mathematical method;
4. input and output evidence;
5. a clear statement of assumptions;
6. impact and limitations.

Unsupported claims, screenshots without reproducible evidence, or demands for payment are not treated as verified findings.
