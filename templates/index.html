How It Works

1

Pixel Encoding

Every image is a sequence of pixels. Each pixel sequence maps to a unique, astronomically large integer.

2

Address Mapping

The integer is encrypted and encoded as a compact base-62 address. Different keys produce different orderings.

3

Reversible

Given any address, the exact image is reconstructed. Given any image, its exact address is computed. Nothing is stored.

4

Complete

Every photograph, painting, and frame of every film exists here — along with every image never before seen by human eyes.
